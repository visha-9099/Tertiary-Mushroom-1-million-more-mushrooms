🍄 Tertiary Mushroom: 1 Million More Mushrooms
This repository features an extensive dataset of over 1 million mushrooms, providing detailed biological, ecological, and morphological characteristics.
It is ideal for mycologists, data scientists, environmental researchers, and enthusiasts looking to explore mushroom classification, toxicity prediction, 
And ecological impact.

📌 Dataset Features
🏷️ Mushroom Characteristics
Scientific Name & Common Name
Genus & Species Classification
Cap Features: Shape, color, texture, diameter.
Gills & Spores: Color, spacing, attachment.
Stipe (Stem) Properties: Height, thickness, texture, color.
Habitat: Forest, grassland, wetland, decaying wood.
Seasonality: Growth season (spring, summer, autumn, winter).
Edibility: Edible, inedible, toxic, psychoactive.
Spore Print Color: Used for species identification.
Chemical Reactions: Response to potassium hydroxide, ammonia, and iron salts.

🔍 Key Insights & Use Cases
🍽️ Edibility & Toxicity Prediction
Classifying mushrooms as safe, toxic, or hallucinogenic using biological features.
Identifying common traits of poisonous mushrooms (e.g., Amanita genus).
Comparing nutritional value of edible species.

🌱 Environmental & Ecological Impact
Mapping mushroom habitats across different regions.
Analyzing how climate change affects mushroom growth.
Studying the role of fungi in soil health and decomposition.
